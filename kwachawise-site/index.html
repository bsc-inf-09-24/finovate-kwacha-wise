Store first. Sort second. Enrich with AI last.

Classification is a deliberate, later decision made by you — never an AI guess made in the background. That keeps every record accurate.

  1. 01

    Capture

    A background listener saves every incoming money SMS the instant it arrives — zero effort, nothing typed.

  2. 02

    Review

    Unsorted transactions wait in one queue. Cash sales with no SMS trail can be logged by hand from the same screen.

  3. 03

    Tag

    Mark each entry 💼 Business or 🏠 Personal, add a short note, done. This is the only manual step in the whole flow.

  4. 04

    Understand

    On demand, tagged records are summarized into a plain financial health signal and three next actions.
